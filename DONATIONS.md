# Supporting WinBridge

WinBridge is free, open source, and made to **make gaming fun on Linux**. There is no paywall and
there never will be for the software itself. If it helps you, here are ways to give back.

## Without spending a cent

- ⭐ **Star the repository** so others can find it.
- 🎮 **Test it with your games and your controller**, then tell us what worked
  ([issues](https://github.com/jamie-codez/winbridge/issues/new/choose)). Real-world reports are the
  most valuable thing you can give us, especially on Fedora, Arch and Debian.
- 🛠 **Contribute** code, docs, translations or artwork: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- 📣 **Tell a friend** who is thinking about trying Linux but worries about their games.
- 🐧 **Support the projects we stand on**: [umu-launcher](https://github.com/Open-Wine-Components/umu-launcher),
  [Wine](https://www.winehq.org), [DXVK](https://github.com/doitsujin/dxvk),
  [VKD3D-Proton](https://github.com/HansKristian-Work/vkd3d-proton) and your distribution.

## Financial support

Money helps with hardware for testing (a spread of GPUs, controllers and distributions), and with
the maintainer's time.

Financial contribution links will be added here as soon as they are set up (for example GitHub
Sponsors). Until a link appears on this page and in the repository's **Sponsor** button, please be
careful: **anything claiming to collect money for WinBridge that is not linked from this
repository is not us.**

## Where support goes

Support is spent on, in order: testing hardware and controllers, infrastructure (CI and release
builds), and maintainer time. We will publish a short note whenever support is received and what
it paid for.

Thank you. Enjoy your games!
